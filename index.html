From discovery to decision

How to use an OrientDig spreadsheet

A useful shortlist preserves the original listing and separates confirmed details from questions. Use these three checks for every candidate.

01 / SOURCE

Keep the original link

Open the CAFANS source page and locate the original marketplace listing. Save the seller, product ID, selected color, size, quantity and date. Ask your chosen agent whether that exact listing can be purchased before funding an order.

02 / FIT & EVIDENCE

Compare measurable details

For clothing, compare equivalent garment measurements in centimeters. For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole length. Record material claims as listing claims until confirmed. Ask for clarification when a size chart or option photo is missing.

03 / FINAL REVIEW

Check before shipping

If warehouse photos are available, match them to the ordered size, color and quantity. Look at visible construction and ask for additional measurements when needed. Confirm any return deadline and the shipping quote before authorizing dispatch.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

An OrientDig spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize marketplace links, selected options, prices and checking notes. This page is an independent guide with a CAFANS catalog selection, rather than an official OrientDig service or a Google Sheet.

Are these products verified for purchase through OrientDig?

No. These are real CAFANS catalog entries. Current OrientDig compatibility, stock, selected options and purchase eligibility have not been verified. Check the original marketplace link with your chosen agent before paying.

Do the displayed prices include shipping?

No. The displayed amounts are estimated USD item prices observed on CAFANS. International shipping, agent fees, payment charges and any applicable taxes are separate. Confirm the current selected-option price and quote before placing an order.

Are the images warehouse QC photos?

The images are sourced from CAFANS product listings and are not presented as independently verified warehouse QC photos. Request photos of your exact ordered item when available. Photographs alone do not establish authenticity, composition or durability.

How do I choose the right clothing size?

Use the listing’s measurement chart and compare it with a garment that fits you. Compare the same measurement methods: a flat chest width differs from a full chest circumference. Save the selected size and ask for missing measurements before ordering.

What should I do when a product link stops working?

Mark the row as unavailable or unconfirmed. Reopen the source page and check the product ID rather than assuming a similar photograph is the same item. Do not reuse an old price or stock note without checking it again.

Is this website operated by OrientDig or CAFANS?

No official affiliation is claimed. This is an independent research page directing visitors to CAFANS. The “OrientDig spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://cafans.com; they do not place an order or open an OrientDig checkout.
